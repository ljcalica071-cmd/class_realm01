# ClassRealm Teacher Demonstration Script

## Demo account

- **Username:** `teacher`
- **Password:** `teacher123`
- **Role:** Teacher

## Opening

1. From the login screen, select the Teacher demo quick-login option, or enter the credentials above.
2. Click **Login** and wait for the Teacher Dashboard.
3. Introduce the dashboard as the teacher's workspace for classes, assessment, attendance, materials, and academic records.

## Demonstration flow

### 1. Dashboard and today's classes

1. Point out the class summary cards for today's classes, students, and upcoming assessments.
2. Show the **Today's Classes** panel with General Mathematics, section, room, time, and attendance status.
3. Open **View All Schedule** to show the seeded Section A and Section B schedule records.

### 2. Classroom setup and students

1. Open **Classroom Setup** and show the academic year, grade level, subject, and sections.
2. Open **Manage Students** and demonstrate the student list, search/filter controls, and student details.
3. If appropriate for the presentation, open the add-student dialog and show the fields without saving a duplicate record.
4. Open **Manage Parents** to show the linked parent directory and search/sort controls.

### 3. Classwork and learning materials

1. Open **Classwork**.
2. Demonstrate the create activity/requirement flow and point out the subject, section, deadline, submission type, and publication settings.
3. Return to the list and show how a teacher can view submissions for an activity.
4. Open **Lesson Planning** and show the existing lesson plans.
5. Open **Learning Materials** and show the uploaded modules, slides, links, and section assignments.

### 4. Attendance, assessment, and grades

1. Open **Attendance** and show the daily attendance records and sync status.
2. Open **Assessment Monitoring** to show quiz/exam monitoring and submission progress.
3. Open **Item Analysis** to show the assessment analysis area.
4. Open **Gradebook** or **Grades** to show section filtering, grading criteria, and student scores.

## Closing

1. Return to **Dashboard** and point out that all teacher workflows are accessible from the categorized sidebar.
2. Select **Logout**.
3. Confirm that the app returns to the login screen.

## Expected result

The audience should see the complete teacher workflow: configure classes, manage learners and parents, prepare instruction, monitor attendance and assessments, and review grades.
