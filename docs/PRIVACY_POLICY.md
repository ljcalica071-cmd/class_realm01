# ClassRealm — Privacy Policy
**Eduardo Barretto Senior Integrated School • Version 2.0.0 (SSHS Edition)**  
**Effective Date:** November 2026  
**Governing Law:** Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) & Google Play Store User Data Policy

---

## 1. Introduction
ClassRealm ("we," "our," or "the System") is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of personal and academic information of all students, parents/guardians, teachers, and administrators at Eduardo Barretto Senior Integrated School (EBSIS). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we process, analyze, and store it, and how users may exercise their rights—including account and data deletion—in strict compliance with Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect only the minimum personal, academic, and security data necessary to deliver official school management and learning services:

* **Students:** Full Name, Learner Reference Number (LRN), Verified Personal or School Email Address, Date of Birth, Gender, Grade Level, and Section.
* **Parents / Legal Guardians:** Full Name, Verified Email Address, Contact/Mobile Number, Relationship to the linked student(s), and linked Student LRN(s).
* **Teachers & Administrators:** Full Name, Email Address, Contact Number, Subject Specialization, Consultation Hours, and optional public faculty directory information.
* **RFID and Attendance Data:** Assigned School RFID Card Identifier, daily time-in/time-out scan timestamps, attendance status (Present, Late, Absent, Excused), and campus/classroom attendance scanner records.
* **Academic, Classwork, and Performance Analytics Data:** Written Works (WW), Performance Tasks (PT), Quarterly Examination (EX) scores, uploaded activity submissions and files, computed initial and transmuted grades (under DepEd Order No. 15, s. 2026), class rankings, learning trajectory indicators, and teacher feedback.
* **Assessment Integrity Telemetry:** During active online quizzes and exams only, the System logs application focus changes (tab switches, window minimize or focus loss, and copy attempts) to uphold examination integrity. ClassRealm does NOT record camera video, microphone audio, personal photos, contacts, or device GPS location.
* **Account & Session Security Metadata:** Device session identifiers, login timestamps, email verification records, and failed authentication counters used solely to prevent unauthorized account access and ensure single-session safety.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
Your data is processed strictly for legitimate educational, institutional, and campus safety purposes:

* Authenticating user identity and providing secure role-based access to the Admin, Teacher, Student, and Parent portals;
* Recording, computing, and reporting daily attendance (including DepEd School Form 2 exports) and quarterly academic grades;
* Generating real-time Performance Analytics to help teachers identify learners who are advancing or who need academic support;
* Delivering real-time attendance warnings, grade releases, and official school broadcast notifications to students and linked parents/guardians;
* Sending One-Time Passwords (OTPs), account verification codes, and security alerts via email;
* Maintaining examination integrity during digital assessments and protecting portal security.

**Commercial Advertising Prohibition:** We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or use student, parent, or teacher personal data for commercial advertising or third-party marketing.

---

## 4. Secure Local Device Storage and Cloud Synchronization
When using the mobile application in areas with weak or intermittent internet connectivity, ClassRealm temporarily stores essential academic and attendance records in secure local storage on your device. Once an internet connection is restored, these records are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections to our secure school cloud system. Logging out of your account immediately clears active session tokens from your device.

---

## 5. Authorized Infrastructure & Data Processing Partners
To ensure reliable, high-availability service for the school community, ClassRealm utilizes trusted enterprise cloud infrastructure for encrypted authentication, database hosting, file storage, and email verification delivery. These services process data strictly on behalf of Eduardo Barretto Senior Integrated School under strict confidentiality and security agreements. Student personal identities (names, LRNs, emails, and grades) are never shared with external marketing entities or unauthorized third parties.

---

## 6. Data Sharing, Visibility, and 18+ Student Privacy Rights
* **Strict Access Control:** Access to student data is strictly restricted to the student themselves, their assigned subject teachers, class adviser, authorized school administrators, and verified linked parents/guardians.
* **Adult Student Privacy Rights (18+):** Under Republic Act No. 10173, students who have reached eighteen (18) years of age and above may independently enable or restrict parent/guardian visibility of their Quarterly Term Grades and Final Grades through the "Parent / Guardian Privacy Controls" in Student Settings. Daily campus attendance and emergency safety notifications remain visible to linked parents/guardians for campus safety.

---

## 7. Data Security Measures
We implement organizational, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your records against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. These include:
* End-to-end encrypted HTTPS/TLS data transmission;
* Secure salted password hashing;
* Strict role-based access restrictions (RBAC);
* One-Time Password (OTP) verification for sensitive operations;
* Single-session login protection;
* Automated rate-limiting against unauthorized brute-force attempts.

---

## 8. Data Retention Policy
Personal and academic records are retained only for the duration of the active Academic School Year and for the mandatory retention period required by Department of Education (DepEd) school record-keeping regulations. Inactive or graduated accounts are archived and purged in accordance with official school administration schedules.

---

## 9. Account and Data Deletion Policy (Google Play & RA 10173 Compliance)
Users (Students, Parents/Guardians, and Teachers) have the right to request the deletion of their ClassRealm user account and associated personal portal data at any time:

* **In-School Request:** Submit a request directly to your Class Adviser (for Students and Parents) or to the School System Administrator / ICT Coordinator at Eduardo Barretto Senior Integrated School.
* **Email Request:** Send an email with the subject line `"ClassRealm Account Deletion Request"` to the ClassRealm System Administrator / Support Team using your registered email address, stating your Full Name, User Role (Student, Parent, or Teacher), and registered Email or LRN.
* **Verification & Timeline:** To protect against unauthorized deletion, the Administrator or Adviser will verify the requester's identity and process the account deletion within seven (7) to fourteen (14) school days.
* **Deleted vs. Retained Records:** Upon approval, your login credentials, portal profile, device session tokens, and personal app access are permanently deleted. Official academic grades and attendance records required by the Department of Education (DepEd) as part of official school registries remain under the custody of Eduardo Barretto Senior Integrated School until the end of the mandatory DepEd retention period.

---

## 10. Your Rights Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)
As a data subject under Philippine law, you are entitled to the following rights:
* **Right to be Informed:** To know how your personal data is collected and used.
* **Right to Access:** To request reasonable access to your personal and academic records stored in the System.
* **Right to Rectification:** To dispute and request correction of any inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete personal information.
* **Right to Object & Erasure/Blocking:** To object to unauthorized processing or request deletion of personal data, subject to mandatory DepEd record-keeping laws.
* **Right to Data Portability:** To request a structured copy of your academic records in accordance with school guidelines.
