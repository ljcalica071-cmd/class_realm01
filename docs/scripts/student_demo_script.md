# ClassRealm Student Demonstration Script

## Demo account

- **Username:** `student`
- **Password:** `student123`
- **Role:** Student

## Opening

1. From the login screen, select the Student demo quick-login option, or enter the credentials above.
2. Click **Login** and wait for the Student Home screen.
3. Introduce the screen as the learner's personal view of school activities, schedule, attendance, requirements, materials, and grades.

## Demonstration flow

### 1. Student home

1. Point out the student name, grade/section, and academic summary.
2. Show the current academic information and any recent activity or notification cards.
3. Explain that the student only sees records connected to the logged-in account and section.

### 2. Attendance and schedule

1. Open **Attendance Records**.
2. Show the attendance history and status labels such as present, late, absent, or excused.
3. Open **Class Schedule**.
4. Show the day-by-day schedule, subject, room, and class time for the student's section.

### 3. Learning materials and requirements

1. Open **Learning Materials**.
2. Show the available modules, presentation files, links, and lesson resources.
3. Open **Academic Requirements**.
4. Show requirement details, deadlines, submission status, and any available submission action.

### 4. Assessments and grades

1. Open the assessment or classwork area and show a published quiz/exam or activity.
2. Explain that the learner can answer a published assessment and that the connectivity option is simulated for the demo.
3. Open **Published Grades** to show released scores and category summaries.
4. Point out the grading components when displayed: Written Works, Performance Task, and Examinations.

### 5. Settings

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Show the learner profile information, LRN, section, and simulated online-status control.
3. Explain that the online-status toggle is for demonstrating quiz/exam connectivity behavior.

## Closing

1. Return to the Student Home screen.
2. Select **Logout**.
3. Confirm that the app returns to the login screen.

## Expected result

The audience should see a learner-focused portal that provides read access to academic information and access to the learner's published activities, requirements, materials, attendance, schedule, and grades.
