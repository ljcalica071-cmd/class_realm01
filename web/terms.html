2. Purpose, Institutional Scope, and Intended Use

ClassRealm is deployed strictly and exclusively for legitimate educational, classroom administration, academic performance evaluation, institutional communication, and campus security purposes within Eduardo Barretto Senior Integrated School.

The System supports four (4) distinct role-based access tiers:

  • School Administrator Portal: Enterprise-level management of school year terms, academic calendars, teacher onboarding, centralized emergency announcements, system audit monitoring, and platform operational health.
  • Teacher Portal: Encoding, computing, and releasing official learner grades, managing classroom attendance sessions, reviewing real-time Performance Analytics and rankings, authoring digital assessments, publishing learning modules, and conducting live anti-cheating supervision.
  • Student Portal: Reviewing personalized class schedules, accessing learning materials, submitting classwork activities and attachments, taking proctored assessments, monitoring attendance scan records, and tracking quarterly academic progress.
  • Parent and Guardian Portal: Read-only monitoring of their linked child's daily campus attendance, official announcements, teacher directory, and authorized academic grades.

Commercial exploitation, external advertising, unauthorized third-party integrations, and any utilization outside sanctioned school operations are strictly prohibited.

3. User Accounts, Access Credentials, Email Integrity, and Role Responsibilities

Access to ClassRealm is restricted to verified members of the Eduardo Barretto Senior Integrated School community. All users are personally responsible for upholding the confidentiality of their credentials and adhering to their assigned roles:

  • Valid Email & Identity Integrity: Users must register and maintain an active, accessible, and legitimate email address. Disposable, temporary, or fabricated email addresses are strictly prohibited and automatically rejected by the System to ensure reliable One-Time Password (OTP) delivery, account verification, and official school communication.
  • Single-Session Protection: To safeguard student and faculty accounts from unauthorized sharing, the System monitors active login sessions and alerts users if conflicting or simultaneous access is detected on multiple devices.
  • System Administrators: Obligated to enforce institutional data protection standards, manage teacher access permissions, safeguard master administrative controls, schedule non-disruptive maintenance windows, and ensure prompt response to data privacy inquiries.
  • Teachers: Must ensure prompt, objective, and accurate recording of attendance, learning outputs, and quarterly grades adhering strictly to Department of Education (DepEd) standards under the Strengthened Senior High School Curriculum (DepEd Order No. 15, s. 2026). Teachers must never share their portal credentials or grant unverified individuals access to student records.
  • Students: Expected to demonstrate unwavering academic honesty during examinations, submit their own original classwork, maintain the security of their login credentials, and use only their personally issued RFID card for campus gate and classroom attendance.
  • Parents and Legal Guardians: Granted non-editable, read-only monitoring access to foster active parental guidance. Parents agree to utilize the portal solely for supporting their child's academic growth and must refrain from attempting to alter records, compromise other student accounts, or impersonate their child during online assessments.

4. RFID Card Attendance, Scanner Terminals, and Anti-Proxy Regulations

  • Physical Card Possession: Every student is issued an individual school RFID card linked to their unique Learner Reference Number (LRN). The card remains the student's personal responsibility throughout the academic school year.
  • Tap Execution: Students must tap only their own card upon arriving at and departing from designated school scanning terminals. Tapping on behalf of an absent or late classmate ("proxy attendance") constitutes a severe breach of school discipline and will result in disciplinary action under the EBSIS Student Code of Conduct.
  • Hardware Tampering: Any attempt to physically damage, disconnect, tamper with, clone, emulate via unauthorized wireless transmitters, or manipulate classroom attendance readers or school network infrastructure is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate administrative sanctions and disciplinary review.

5. Academic Integrity and Real-Time Anti-Cheating Proctoring Safeguards

  • Activity and Focus Monitoring: During active assessment sessions, the System monitors and records application focus events. This includes detecting tab switches, browser focus loss, window minimization, split-screen multi-tasking, backgrounding the application, and unauthorized copy/paste attempts ("suspicious session events").
  • Proctor Alerts and Automated Enforcement: Detected anomalies are transmitted in real time to the proctoring teacher's monitoring console. Based on the frequency and severity of detected anomalies, the teacher or the System may automatically issue on-screen warnings, temporarily lock the assessment session, deduct score points, or terminate the student's exam attempt, requiring a formal supervised retake.
  • Strict Privacy Guarantee: ClassRealm does NOT record camera feeds, microphone audio, screen video recordings, browser history outside the assessment page, personal photo files, or device GPS locations. The monitoring safeguard is operational strictly while an assessment attempt is in progress.

6. Adult Student Grade Privacy Rights (18 Years Old and Above)

In strict compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), students who have reached the age of majority (eighteen [18] years old and above) possess independent statutory privacy rights over their sensitive educational records:

  • Privacy Controls: Adult learners are provided with configurable "Parent / Guardian Privacy Controls" within their Student Settings screen, allowing them to enable or restrict parent/guardian visibility over their Quarterly Term Grades and Final Computed Grades.
  • Non-Restricted Safety Records: Daily campus attendance timestamps, emergency broadcast notifications, class schedules, and institutional announcements remain continuously accessible to linked parents/guardians regardless of the student's age, in accordance with the school's duty of care, campus safety policies, and institutional parental communication guidelines.
